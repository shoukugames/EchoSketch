# Changelog

## v0.1.1 — 2026-11-06

- **Japanese / 日本語:** the whole game can now be played in Japanese, voice included.
  Every screen, sound name and level title is translated, answers are spelled kana by kana,
  and 150 new Japanese voice clips were added. Switch with the 日本語 / English button on the
  title screen (or the L key). The choice is saved; on first start the game follows the
  computer's language.
- Player names can be typed with a Japanese input method (IME).
- Bundled the rounded Japanese font M PLUS Rounded 1c (SIL OFL).
- **New voice:** all English speech is re-recorded with Kokoro (voice af_heart), the same
  engine as BeatClap, for clearer words and letter names. It replaces Piper TTS.
- Long button labels now shrink to fit.

## v0.1.0 — 2026-11-05

First release.

- 51 real-world sounds in six groups: farm, wild animals, around the house, things that go,
  nature and music.
- 8 levels that get harder: less familiar sounds, more look-alike choices, and hidden words
  on the last two levels.
- Four cartoon picture choices per sound, with a 99-second timer for each round.
- Every answer is spoken, then spelled letter by letter with each letter highlighted.
- When time runs out, the answer is shown, spoken and spelled, and the child can move on.
- Several player profiles, each with its own buddy picture and stars.
- Grown-ups area, opened by press-and-hold: overview, a list of tricky sounds showing which
  pictures were chosen by mistake, recent answers, practice mode, and reset/delete.
- Sounds a child has missed come up more often in later games.
- Original relaxing ambient music that quietly dips under speech and clue sounds.
- Credits screen with creators, release date, website, email, GPL-3.0 notice and full
  attribution for every asset.
- Linux `.deb` package and Windows single-file `.exe`.
